## DRF's constitutional role

DRF is the convergence/documentation layer that records:
- runtime identities
- authority boundaries
- dependencies
- contracts
- registry participation
- replay/evidence/observability responsibilities
- knowledge contribution

DRF does **not** become:
- the Sovereign Core
- RAJYA
- DGIC
- a universal execution engine
- a universal registry
- a governance authority

## Plug-and-play principle

A participant is considered plug-and-play only when:
1. its identity is unique,
2. authority is explicit,
3. upstream/downstream relationships are known,
4. contracts are documented,
5. registry participation is defined,
6. evidence/replay/observability responsibilities are defined,
7. unresolved dependencies are explicitly surfaced.
